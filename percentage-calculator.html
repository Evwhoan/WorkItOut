How this calculator works

Use the first result for discounts and tax, the second to see what share one number is of another, and the third for increases and decreases.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out a percentage discount?

Enter the discount as Number A and the original price as Number B. The large result is the amount taken off. Subtract it from the price to get the sale price.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Enter the old value as Number A and the new value as Number B. The percentage change line shows the increase, or a negative figure for a decrease.
