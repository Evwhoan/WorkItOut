How this calculator works

This uses the standard repayment formula, where each payment covers interest plus part of the loan. Real mortgages may include fees, and fixed deals reset after a set term, so treat the result as a guide.

Frequently asked questions

How much deposit do I need for a mortgage?

Most UK lenders want at least 5% of the property price, but a bigger deposit usually unlocks lower interest rates. Try different deposits above to see how the monthly payment and total interest change.

Does this calculator include fees or rate changes?

No. It assumes one fixed rate for the whole term. Real mortgages often have introductory periods and may add arrangement fees, so use the result as a guide and ask a lender or broker for a personal quote.
