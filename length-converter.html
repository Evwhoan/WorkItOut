Frequently asked questions

How many kilometres are in a mile?

One mile is 1.609344 km. Choose mile as the From unit and km as the To unit to convert any distance.

How many centimetres are in an inch?

One inch is exactly 2.54 cm. Choose inch and cm above to convert any measurement.
