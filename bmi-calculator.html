How this calculator works

BMI is a rough screening measure and does not account for muscle, age or body shape. Speak to a health professional about your own situation. Adult ranges: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30 and over obese.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults the NHS describes 18.5 to 24.9 as the healthy range. 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or more is obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It does not account for muscle, age, ethnicity or where fat is carried. Treat it as a starting point and speak to a GP or health professional about your own health.
