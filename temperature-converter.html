How this calculator works

Water freezes at 0°C (32°F) and boils at 100°C (212°F). A typical UK oven at gas mark 4 is about 180°C.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 180°C is 356°F, a common oven setting.

At what temperature does water boil?

At sea level water boils at 100°C, which is 212°F or 373.15 K.
