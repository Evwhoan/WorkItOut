How this calculator works

The standard UK VAT rate is 20%, with 5% and 0% rates for some goods. Check HMRC for the rate that applies to your product.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard UK VAT rate?

The standard rate has been 20%, with a reduced rate of 5% and a zero rate for some goods and services. Check GOV.UK for the rate that applies to what you sell.

How do I work out a price before VAT?

Choose Remove VAT, then enter the VAT-inclusive price and the rate. The calculator divides by 1.2 for a 20% rate and shows the VAT portion separately.
