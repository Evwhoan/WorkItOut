How this calculator works

Enter the amount, the yearly interest rate (APR) and how long you will take to repay. Longer terms lower the monthly payment but raise the total interest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between APR and the interest rate?

The interest rate is the cost of borrowing the money. APR also includes mandatory fees, which makes it better for comparing offers. Enter the rate shown in your offer for the closest estimate.

How can I pay less interest on a loan?

A shorter term or a lower rate cuts the total interest. Paying extra when you can may help too, but check for early repayment charges first.
