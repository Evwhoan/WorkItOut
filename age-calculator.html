How this calculator works

Enter a date of birth to see your age in years, months and days, plus the total days lived.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?

The calculator counts full years since your date of birth, then the extra months and days up to today, using the date on your device.

Can I find out how many days old I am?

Yes. The results show your total days lived and the days remaining until your next birthday.
