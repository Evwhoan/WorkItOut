How this calculator works

Interest is compounded monthly and deposits are added at the end of each month. Real returns vary and are not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original money and on the interest already added. Over long periods this snowball effect makes savings grow faster than simple interest.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. Savings rates change and investment returns rise and fall, so treat the figure as an illustration rather than a promise.
